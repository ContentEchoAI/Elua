# Hummingbird AI Output Quality Audit

Goal: Make Hummingbird AI outputs feel premium, specific, useful, and worth saving.

The output should not feel like generic AI text. It should feel like a strategist, copywriter, and growth marketer built a usable plan.

## Quality Standard

Every result should pass these checks:

1. Specific to the user's business, niche, platform, and goal
2. Immediately usable without heavy editing
3. Clear enough for a beginner to act on
4. Specific enough that it does not feel generic
5. Practical enough to create leads, sales, growth, or saved value
6. Strong enough that the user thinks, "I would save this"
7. Premium enough that the user understands why Hummingbird could be paid

## Red Flags

Mark any output that has:

- Generic phrases like "valuable insights", "comprehensive guide", "unlock success", "level up", "start your journey"
- Weak CTAs
- Generic lead magnets
- Fake-sounding claims
- Vague strategy
- Content that feels like a summary instead of a usable post/script
- Offers that do not match the business model
- Viral hooks that are not actually scroll-stopping
- Repeated patterns across different businesses
- Output that sounds like ChatGPT filler
